# Idea: a shorter demo form

We want to cut the demo request form from nine fields to four: name, work email, company and role. Sales thinks the long form scares people off.

- **Metric:** demo form conversion. It sits at 3.1% over the last 90 days.
- **Traffic:** about 9,000 visitors to the demo page in 30 days.
- **Split:** the page tool can split visitors at random, 50/50.
- **Budget:** $6K for the page tool and build time.
- **Value:** a demo request is worth about $400 to us.
- **What we expect:** a 35% lift.
- **Launch:** Feb 1, 2027.

**If it wins:** roll the four-field form out to every demo page and move the $6K test budget into paid search for the demo page.
**If it loses:** keep the nine-field form and put the $6K into the webinar program.
**If it is in between:** run 30 more days on the same split, with $3K more for the page tool.
**If it is doing harm at day 15:** marketing ops switches every visitor back to the nine-field form the same day.

Owners: Lena Park runs demand gen. Omar Reyes runs marketing ops.
