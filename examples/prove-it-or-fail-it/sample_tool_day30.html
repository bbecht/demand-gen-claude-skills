How the numbers work

The standard

95% sure, for every test. A Scale call is a fluke about 1 time in 20. The test is sized to catch a real lift of the success size 8 times in 10.

The success number

The largest of: the smallest lift 30 days can detect, the lift that pays back the budget, and for before and after, the business's own swing over the last 12 weeks.

The kill number

At day 30, a result at or below it means even the top of the range is under the success number.

The harm line

At day 15, a result at or below it means the whole range is under zero. The idea is doing harm. A slow start never stops a test.

The seal

Every number and agreed action is sealed at design. An edited card cannot be read.
