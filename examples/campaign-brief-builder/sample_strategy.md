# Brasswick: Close the Books Faster

Pipeline target: $1.2M by March 31, 2027.

Audience: US manufacturers with 200 to 1,000 employees that run NetSuite or Sage Intacct and process more than 2,000 supplier invoices a month. Buying roles: CFO, Controller, AP manager.

Offer: The Month-End Close Benchmark, a 20-minute read-out that shows where a manufacturer's close loses days, built from the buyer's own AP numbers. CTA: book a close review.

Message: Brasswick cuts invoice approval from 9 days to 2 for manufacturers on NetSuite or Sage Intacct, so the books close on day 4. Proof: three named manufacturing customers closed on day 4 within two quarters of go-live.

Channels ($90K total):
- LinkedIn ads, $40,000. Job title and company size targeting on CFO, Controller and AP manager at the account list. Cost per lead $200.
- Webinar on Zoom, $15,000. Co-hosted with a NetSuite partner and promoted to the partner's finance list. Cost per lead $100.
- Content syndication through NetLine, $20,000. Leads filtered to finance titles at manufacturers of 200 to 1,000 employees. Cost per lead $80.
- SDR outbound in Outreach, $15,000. Two SDRs call and email Controllers and AP managers on the account list. Cost per lead $150.

Timing: launch January 11, 2027, run 10 weeks.

Funnel, from Salesforce, last four quarters: average deal $30,000. 8% of leads become opportunities. A lead takes 3 weeks to become an opportunity.

Sales: 3 AEs, each can take 10 new opportunities a month. Win rate 25%, sales cycle 90 days.

Owners: ads Priya Nand, demand gen. Content Tom Ellery. Operations Dana Ruiz, RevOps. Sales enablement Marcus Webb, sales director. Creative Northfield Studio (agency). No PR in this campaign.

Tracking: every lead gets the Salesforce campaign brasswick-q1 on creation. Opportunities created within 90 days of a campaign touch take it as primary campaign source.

Risk: the webinar depends on one customer speaker. If she drops out, the webinar has no proof point. Owner: Tom Ellery.
