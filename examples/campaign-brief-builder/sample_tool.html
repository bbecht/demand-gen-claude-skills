How the numbers work

The reverse funnel

Opportunities needed = target divided by average deal size, rounded up. Leads needed = opportunities needed divided by the share of leads that become opportunities, rounded up. Each channel buys its budget divided by its cost per lead, in whole leads.

Timing

Leads are spread evenly across the run weeks. Each week's leads count on the week's last day and become opportunities after your days from lead to opportunity. Check 2 asks whether that falls on or before the due date.

Opportunities with a decimal

They are expected values. 471 leads at 8% is 37.7 opportunities on average.

The verdict

Any Fail: Not ready, and the brief is blocked. An assumed number: Untested. Flags only: Ready with flags. All ten pass: Ready.

Not a forecast

Every number is a projection at your own rates. No benchmarks are used.
